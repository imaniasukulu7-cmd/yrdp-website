Play, Learn and Lead: 150+ Children Together

Community Activity

Creating Friendship Through Play

YRDP recently brought together more than 150 children from different nationalities living in Kakuma for Play, Learn and Lead.

The activity created space for children to play together, learn from one another and develop new relationships across different backgrounds.

For YRDP, the activity demonstrated how simple community activities can create opportunities for connection, participation and learning.
