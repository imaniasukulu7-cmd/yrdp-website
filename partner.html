PARTNERSHIPS

Let's Create Opportunities Together

We welcome organizations, foundations, sports networks, community groups and individuals who share our commitment to children, youth and inclusive communities.

WHY PARTNER WITH YRDP

Partnership That Connects to Community

YRDP works at community level, creating practical opportunities for children and young people through education, sport, leadership, inclusion and empowerment.

01

Community Reach

Work with a youth-led organization connected to communities in Kakuma and Kalobeyei.

02

Youth-Led Action

Support initiatives shaped around the participation and experiences of young people.

03

Inclusive Participation

YRDP also works with persons with disabilities to promote inclusion and meaningful participation.

04

Sport for Development

Use sport and play as practical platforms for connection, learning, teamwork and leadership.

PARTNERSHIP OPPORTUNITIES

Ways We Can Work Together

01

Program Support

Support community activities that provide opportunities for children and young people to learn, play and lead.

02

Sport Partnerships

Collaborate on football and other sporting activities that bring people together.

03

Financial Support

Provide funding for community initiatives, youth programs and inclusive activities.

04

Technical Support

Share expertise, training, mentorship, equipment or other technical resources.

05

Strategic Collaboration

Develop joint initiatives with organizations working toward shared community goals.

06

Equipment & Resources

Support activities through sports equipment, learning materials and other useful resources.

CURRENT OPPORTUNITY

Community Football Tournament

YRDP is planning a community football tournament that aims to bring people from almost all backgrounds across Kakuma together.

The initiative will use football as a platform for teamwork, friendship, inclusion and positive interaction across communities.
