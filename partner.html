PARTNER WITH US

Let's Create Opportunities Together

YRDP welcomes partnerships that support young people, strengthen communities and expand opportunities through education, sport, leadership, inclusion and peacebuilding.

WAYS TO PARTNER

Work With YRDP

Partnerships can take different forms depending on shared goals, community needs and available resources.

01

Program Partnerships

Collaborate with us to design and implement activities that create meaningful opportunities for children and young people.

02

Funding & Grants

Support youth-led initiatives and community programs through grants, small funding opportunities or project support.

03

Technical Support

Share knowledge, expertise, training and resources that strengthen the capacity of young people and community facilitators.

04

Sport for Development

Partner with us to use sport and play as tools for peace, inclusion, learning, leadership and social connection.

05

Community Collaboration

Work alongside YRDP and local communities to respond to challenges and create locally driven solutions.

06

Knowledge & Learning

Exchange experiences, document learning and build stronger approaches to youth-led development.

WHY YRDP

Locally Led. Youth Driven. Community Focused.

YRDP works directly with young people and communities in Kakuma and Kalobeyei, creating practical opportunities for participation, leadership and positive change.
