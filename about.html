ABOUT YRDP

Young People Creating Positive Change

A youth-led organization working with communities to create meaningful opportunities for children and young people.

WHO WE ARE

Community-Led Work Driven by Young People

Youth Relief and Development Program (YRDP) is a youth-led organization working with children, young people and communities in Kakuma and Kalobeyei.

Our work focuses on creating practical opportunities for young people to learn, participate, develop leadership skills and contribute positively to their communities.

We believe that young people should not only be beneficiaries of development initiatives. They should also have meaningful opportunities to participate, lead and shape solutions within their communities.

OUR VISION

Communities Where Young People Can Thrive

We envision communities where children and young people have opportunities to learn, participate, build relationships, develop leadership and contribute to positive change.

OUR MISSION

Creating Opportunities That Matter

YRDP works alongside communities to create inclusive opportunities through education, sport, leadership development, peacebuilding, disability inclusion and livelihoods.

01

Youth Participation

Creating spaces where young people can participate meaningfully in community activities and decision-making.

02

Inclusion

Promoting participation of people from diverse backgrounds, including persons with disabilities.

03

Community Connection

Bringing people together through shared activities that encourage friendship, understanding and peaceful interaction.

OUR APPROACH

Educate. Play. Lead. Include. Empower.

01

Educate

Support learning, knowledge and life-skills development.

02

Play

Use sport and play to build relationships, teamwork and participation.

03

Support young people to develop leadership and take positive community action.

04

Include

Promote meaningful participation of persons with disabilities and diverse communities.

05

Empower

Strengthen people's capacity to participate and create positive change.

INCLUSION & COMMUNITY

Everyone Deserves A Place to Participate

YRDP works with people from different backgrounds and recognizes the importance of creating spaces where everyone can participate meaningfully.

This includes working with persons with disabilities to strengthen inclusion and ensure that community activities provide opportunities for participation.
