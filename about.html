ABOUT YRDP

Young People Creating Positive Change

Youth Relief and Development Program is a youth-led organization creating meaningful opportunities for children and young people through education, sport, leadership, inclusion and community development.

WHO WE ARE

Local Leadership. Meaningful Opportunities.

Youth Relief and Development Program (YRDP) is a youth-led organization working with children, young people, refugee and host communities in Kakuma and Kalobeyei.

We create opportunities for young people to learn, play, lead and participate positively in their communities. Our work brings together education, sport, leadership, inclusion, peacebuilding and livelihoods through practical, community-based activities.

We believe young people are not only beneficiaries of development. They are active leaders, partners and contributors to stronger, more peaceful and inclusive communities.

OUR MISSION

Creating Opportunities That Matter

To create inclusive opportunities that empower children and young people to learn, lead, build relationships and contribute to positive community development.

OUR VISION

Communities Where Young People Can Thrive

Communities where every young person has the opportunity to learn, participate, lead and thrive in a peaceful, inclusive and supportive environment.

WHAT GUIDES US

Our Core Values

Our work is guided by values that place young people, inclusion and community at the centre of everything we do.

Inclusion

We create spaces where children and young people from different backgrounds can participate, contribute and belong.

Peace

We use positive interaction, play and community engagement to strengthen relationships and peaceful coexistence.

Leadership

We support young people to develop confidence, responsibility and the ability to lead positive change.

Participation

We believe meaningful change happens when communities actively participate in shaping solutions.

Learning

We create opportunities for learning through education, sport, play and practical experiences.

Community

We work alongside communities and partners to strengthen local ownership, collaboration and action.

INCLUSION & COMMUNITY

Everyone Deserves A Place to Participate

YRDP works with people from different backgrounds and recognizes the importance of creating spaces where everyone can participate meaningfully.

We work with persons with disabilities, young women, girls, refugee communities and host communities to strengthen inclusion and participation in community activities.
