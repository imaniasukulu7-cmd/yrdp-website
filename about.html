ABOUT YRDP

Young People Creating Positive Change

A youth-led organization working with communities to create meaningful opportunities for children and young people.

WHO WE ARE

Local Leadership. Meaningful Opportunities.

Youth Relief and Development Program (YRDP) is a youth-led organization working with children, young people, refugee and host communities in Kakuma and Kalobeyei.

We create opportunities for young people to learn, play, lead and participate positively in their communities. Our approach brings together education, sport, leadership, inclusion and peacebuilding through practical, community-based activities.

We believe young people are not only beneficiaries of development. They are active leaders and partners in creating stronger, more peaceful and inclusive communities.

01 OUR MISSION

Creating Inclusive Opportunities

To create inclusive opportunities that empower children and young people to learn, lead, build relationships and contribute to positive community development.

02 OUR VISION

Communities Where Young People Thrive

Communities where every young person has the opportunity to learn, participate, lead and thrive.

Our Core Values

Our work is guided by values that place young people, inclusion and community at the centre.

01

Inclusion

We create spaces where children and young people from different backgrounds can participate and belong.

02

03

OUR APPROACH

Educate. Play. Lead. Include. Empower.

Our approach uses practical activities to help young people develop skills, build relationships, participate and take positive action in their communities.

01

Educate

Support learning, knowledge and life-skills development.

02

Play

Use sport and play to build relationships, teamwork and participation.

03

Lead

Support young people to develop leadership and take positive community action.

04

Include

Promote meaningful participation of persons with disabilities and diverse communities.

05

Empower

Strengthen people's capacity to participate and create positive change.

INCLUSION & COMMUNITY

Everyone Deserves A Place to Participate

YRDP works with people from different backgrounds and recognizes the importance of creating spaces where everyone can participate meaningfully.

This includes working with persons with disabilities to strengthen inclusion and ensure that community activities provide opportunities for participation.
