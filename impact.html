IMPACT IN ACTION

Play Can Create Connection

Through Play, Learn and Lead, YRDP brought together more than 150 children from different nationalities living in Kakuma.

The activity created an opportunity for children to interact with people they may not normally meet, participate in games, learn together and develop new relationships.

For YRDP, these moments matter because community development is also about creating spaces where people can connect, participate and feel included.

SPORT FOR COMMUNITY

Football as a Platform for Connection

YRDP is planning a community football tournament designed to bring people from almost every background across Kakuma together.

Beyond competition, the tournament aims to create opportunities for teamwork, friendship, inclusion and positive interaction among community members.
