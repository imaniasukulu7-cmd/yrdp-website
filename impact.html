OUR STORY OF CHANGE

Building Peace Through Participation

YRDP creates safe spaces where young people from refugee and host communities learn together, play together and become leaders within their communities.

Through sport, dialogue, education and youth-led initiatives, participants strengthen relationships, develop confidence and contribute to peaceful coexistence across diverse communities.

Young people are at the centre of the change.

We believe meaningful impact happens when young people have opportunities to participate, build relationships and take positive action in their communities.

WHERE CHANGE HAPPENS

Our Impact Areas

Our work contributes to positive change across several connected areas of community life.

01

Leadership

Young people become mentors, facilitators and community leaders, developing confidence and responsibility through practical participation.

02

Peace

Sport, dialogue and shared activities strengthen relationships, trust and peaceful interaction across diverse communities.

03

Inclusion

Girls, boys, young people from diverse backgrounds and persons with disabilities are supported to participate meaningfully.

04

Learning

Education, practical skills, sport and play create opportunities for young people to learn, develop confidence and explore new possibilities.

05

Community Connection

Shared activities bring people together, helping participants build friendships, understanding and stronger community relationships.

06

Participation

Young people and communities are encouraged to take an active role in shaping activities and contributing to solutions that affect their communities.

LEARNING & ACCOUNTABILITY

We Learn From Every Activity

YRDP uses participation records, attendance tracking, activity reports, participant feedback, stories and testimonies to understand what is working and where improvements are needed.

This helps us strengthen our programs, listen to participants and ensure that our activities remain relevant to the communities we serve.

01

Participation

Tracking who participates and how communities engage in activities.

02

Feedback

Listening to participants and community members to improve our activities.

03

Stories of Change

Documenting experiences and stories that help us understand the human side of our work.
