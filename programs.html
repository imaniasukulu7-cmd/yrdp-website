OUR WORK

Creating Opportunities for Young People to Learn, Play and Lead

YRDP works with refugee and host community youth in Kakuma and Kalobeyei through inclusive programs that strengthen leadership, learning, peace, skills and participation.

OUR APPROACH

Educate. Play. Lead.

We create practical spaces where young people can learn, participate, build relationships and take leadership in their communities.

WHAT WE DO

Our Areas of Work

01

Youth Leadership & Civic Engagement

We support young people to develop leadership, communication and civic engagement skills through mentorship, leadership workshops and youth-led initiatives.

02

Digital Skills & Innovation

We create opportunities for young people to develop digital literacy, access online learning and explore innovation and problem-solving.

03

Entrepreneurship & Economic Empowerment

We support youth through entrepreneurship, financial literacy, skills development and income-generation opportunities.

04

Sport, Culture & Peacebuilding

We use inclusive sport, cultural activities and cooperative games to strengthen relationships, teamwork, peace and social cohesion.

05

Inclusion & Protection

We promote participation and safer spaces through gender inclusion, disability inclusion, safeguarding and child-friendly approaches.

06

Community Peacebuilding

We support community dialogue, peace campaigns, youth peace initiatives and activities that encourage understanding across diverse communities.

WHO WE WORK WITH

Young People at the Centre

Our programs are designed to create meaningful opportunities for young people from diverse backgrounds.

Refugee Youth

Young people from diverse refugee communities.

Host Communities

Young people and community members from surrounding host communities.

Young Women & Girls

Creating opportunities for meaningful participation and leadership.

Persons with Disabilities

Promoting accessible and inclusive participation.

WHERE WE WORK

Kakuma Camp and Kalobeyei settlement

Kakuma Refugee Camp is YRDP's main operations hub, with program activities and outreach extending to Kalobeyei Settlement.

Youth Relief and Development Program

Creating opportunities for children and young people through education, sport, leadership, inclusion and community development.
