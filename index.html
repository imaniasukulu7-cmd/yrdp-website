WHO WE ARE

Local Leadership. Meaningful Opportunities.

YRDP is a youth-led organization working alongside refugee and host communities to create opportunities for children and young people.

We use education, sport, leadership, inclusion and community-based activities to help young people develop skills, build relationships and participate positively in their communities.

SPORT FOR PEACE

Bringing Kakuma Together Through Football

YRDP is planning a community football tournament designed to bring people from different backgrounds across Kakuma together through sport.

The tournament will use football as a platform for friendship, teamwork, inclusion and peaceful interaction among refugee and host communities.
