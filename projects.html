OUR PROJECTS

Turning Ideas Into Community Action

Explore initiatives that create opportunities for children, young people and communities through play, learning, sport and leadership.

FEATURED INITIATIVE

Play, Learn and Lead

Creating safe and inclusive opportunities for children to connect, learn and participate through play.

The activity brought together more than 150 children from different nationalities living in Kakuma. Through structured play, learning and leadership activities, children had opportunities to interact with others, build relationships and develop confidence.

UPCOMING COMMUNITY INITIATIVE

Community Football Tournament

YRDP is planning a community football tournament that will bring people from different backgrounds across Kakuma together through sport.

The tournament will create opportunities for friendship, teamwork, inclusion and positive interaction while using football as a platform for community connection.
